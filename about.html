Our Story

Every micron
should have a purpose.

ANVIX™ is the agricultural films brand of Blacksoil International Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia. We believe practical growing performance and a more thoughtful use of materials can belong in the same conversation.

We started with the field.

Commercial growers face practical challenges: maintaining bed coverage, managing weeds, choosing appropriate thickness and buying on a workable supply schedule. Our role is to understand the application first, then discuss suitable film options with our manufacturing partners.

Today our focus is mulch film. The brand has room to develop relevant agricultural film solutions as customer needs become clearer.

Material efficiency

Where fit for purpose, thinner structures can reduce PE material by weight. Actual field suitability still matters.

Practical supply

We engage commercial growers and distributors about their specifications, schedules and procurement requirements.

Responsible direction

We seek better information and documented alternatives for agricultural plastic end-of-use challenges.
