Towards a More
Sustainable Future

Around the world, agriculture is evolving towards more sustainable farming practices. Governments, food producers and growers are placing greater emphasis on reducing plastic pollution, protecting soil health and meeting increasingly stringent environmental regulations and ESG commitments. At the same time, international standards for soil biodegradable mulch films continue to advance, supporting the transition to more sustainable agricultural solutions. ANVIX™ Biodegradable Mulch Films are developed to help growers maintain crop performance while reducing plastic waste and supporting healthier soils for future cultivation.

Less Reliance on Herbicides

By creating a protective barrier over the soil, mulch films help suppress weed growth and reduce the need for chemical herbicide applications. This supports more efficient weed management while helping growers minimise chemical intervention throughout the growing season.
