Product philosophy

Strength Where
It Matters.

Not every growing cycle calls for a lightweight film.

Some applications demand additional thickness to withstand installation, field handling and extended exposure to outdoor growing conditions.

ANVIX™ Durable Series offers heavier-gauge polyethylene mulch films for growers who prioritise mechanical resilience and film integrity throughout their intended growing cycle.

Key Performance Considerations

Tensile Strength

Designed to accommodate pulling and mechanical stresses during film laying, installation and field handling.

Elongation at Break

Flexible film construction helps accommodate stretching and deformation under tension.

Dart Impact Resistance

Film thickness and material selection can contribute to greater resistance against sudden impact and puncture-related damage.

Tear Resistance

Weathering Stability

UV stabilisation can help preserve film properties during outdoor exposure when appropriately formulated for the intended growing conditions.

Choosing the right thickness

The Right Gauge for
Every Growing Cycle.

Film thickness plays an important role in mechanical strength, handling resistance and material usage.

From medium-gauge to heavier-duty options, ANVIX™ Durable Series offers a selection of thicknesses to match different growing requirements.

Our approach focuses on selecting the appropriate film specification for each application, rather than simply choosing the thickest available option.
