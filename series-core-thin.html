Core Thin.
Less material. Purposeful coverage.

Core Thin is a material-conscious conventional PE direction for applications where a lower film thickness can still meet practical growing and handling requirements.

A smarter use of material

The Right
Thickness Matters.

More material isn't always necessary. Core Thin focuses on lightweight film constructions suited to growing applications where heavier-gauge mulch may not be required.

A practical approach to material efficiency, without losing sight of the film's intended purpose.

Why Core Thin?

Less Material.
More Purpose.

Material Efficiency

Uses less polyethylene per square metre than thicker films of comparable construction.

Fit for Purpose

Select film thickness according to crop cycle, growing conditions and application needs.

Lightweight Handling

Lower roll weight at comparable dimensions can make transport and field handling easier.

Material & Construction

Built Around
the Essentials.

Built for Strength, Flexibility & Material Efficiency.

ANVIX™ Core Thin features a three-layer co-extruded polyethylene construction, combining three layers into a single lightweight film.

This multi-layer structure allows different material properties to be balanced across the film, supporting mechanical performance while optimising material usage for selected growing applications.

Performance

Key Performance Benefits

Enhanced Flexibility & Elongation

Engineered to provide flexibility and stretching capability, helping the film withstand tension during installation and field handling.

Dart Impact Resistance

Layer construction and resin selection can improve resistance to sudden impact, helping reduce the risk of damage during handling and application.

Balanced Tear Resistance

Designed to achieve a suitable balance of tear resistance in both machine and transverse directions.

Optimised Film Strength

Multi-layer engineering allows mechanical properties to be tailored to the intended application and film thickness.
