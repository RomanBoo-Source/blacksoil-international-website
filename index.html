Why ANVIX™ exists

We believe agricultural films deserve a more thoughtful approach. Not just more products. Not just more plastic. But better judgement around what each application actually needs.

Today, ANVIX™ focuses on mulch films. Tomorrow, the brand can grow into other relevant agricultural film and crop protection solutions — without losing its core philosophy.

As environmental expectations rise and ESG conversations become more relevant across supply chains, we see value in two parallel directions: lighter conventional films where suitable, and stronger attention to soil-biodegradable alternatives where they fit.

Our film philosophy

Not every application needs more material. Some need better material thinking.

Conventional PE

Lighter, when the application allows.

For selected conventional mulch film applications, thinner structures can reduce polymer consumption and make rolls lighter to handle — provided the film still matches the crop cycle, installation method and required field performance.

Thoughtful materials, practical use
Soil-biodegradable

A stronger emphasis on what comes next.

We see soil-biodegradable mulch films as an important direction for suitable growing applications, especially where end-of-season film removal and disposal create challenges for growers.
