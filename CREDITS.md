# ANVIX website image provenance and credits

This project includes remote links to **real third-party photographs** hosted on Wikimedia Commons. They are illustrative stock/commons photographs of fields or films, not photos of ANVIX customers, factories or ANVIX field testing. Internet access is needed for the live photos. Each visual also includes an **original, manually generated SVG landscape illustration** that acts as a fallback; the SVGs are not real photographs or field evidence.

- Hero: **Vegetables grown on plastic mulch**, source US Department of Agriculture (US government public domain), https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vegetables_grown_on_plastic_mulch.JPG
- Core Thin: **Strawberry plants growing along black plastic mulch in a farm field during springtime**, Shixart1985, CC BY 2.0; image visually cropped by CSS. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strawberry_plants_growing_along_black_plastic_mulch_in_a_farm_field_during_springtime.jpg
- Durable: **Plastic mulched chilli field**, Srimathiv1995, CC BY-SA 4.0; visually cropped by CSS. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Plastic_mulched_chilli_field.jpg
- Biodegradable: **Mulch Film made of PLA-Blend Bio-Flex**, F. Kesselring / FKuR Willich, CC BY-SA 3.0 DE; example of another company's product, NOT ANVIX product and NOT ANVIX's certification. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mulch_Film_made_of_PLA-Blend_Bio-Flex.jpg
- Reflective: **Strawberry field with plastic**, Downtowngal, CC BY-SA 3.0; visually cropped by CSS. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strawberry_field_with_plastic.jpg
- Grower / partnerships: **Strawberry Fields In Carlsbad Ca**, Wikimedia Commons; license and attribution should be rechecked before deployment. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Strawberry_Fields_In_Carlsbad_Ca_(120852173).jpeg

Before publishing, confirm exact original image URLs and license requirements, preserve credits / notices and replace third-party imagery with authorized ANVIX product / grower photographs when possible. These images must not be described as local Malaysian plantations or proof of ANVIX test results.

## STTOKE imagery
STTOKE's website features product-focused lifestyle imagery. We cannot establish from viewing it alone whether every part of those images is photographic or AI-enhanced; do not copy its photography without permission. This design borrows broad visual principles, not STTOKE's assets.
