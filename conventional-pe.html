Polyethylene mulch film options for commercial growers, plantations and distributors. A film should meet the intended crop cycle and field practices without using unnecessary material.

Conventional PE is our foundation.

Selected conventional grades are sourced in three-layer co-extruded structures using virgin-grade polyethylene. The specific grade, resin composition and performance are to be confirmed for each order.

Our film selection considers installation, ultraviolet exposure, thickness, film color, perforation and practical service-life requirements.
